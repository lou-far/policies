# Hurdle Turtles – Privacy Policy

*Effective date:* 22 October 2026

Hurdle Turtles ("the game") is made by loufar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the
game uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is suitable for all ages, and we treat every player
as a child.

## The short version
- We do not collect, see or store any of your personal information.
- The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics.
- Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device.
- The game shows adverts from Google AdMob. Every advert request is marked as directed at children:
  adverts are limited to content suitable for general audiences and are never personalised.
- Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## Information saved on your device
The game saves the following on your device only, so it can remember your progress and preferences:
- your best score;
- your settings (sound effects, music, reduced motion and vibration);
- whether you have seen the "how to play" screen;
- any extra lives you have bought, and whether you have bought Remove Ads;
- a record of purchases already given to you, so none is given twice;
- how many games you have finished, so full-screen adverts are not shown too often.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in
your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

## Adverts
The game shows a full-screen advert from time to time, and offers an optional advert you can choose to
watch for an extra life. These adverts are provided by *Google AdMob*.

Because the game is suitable for children, every advert request is marked as *child-directed* and as
coming from a user *under the age of consent, and adverts are limited to the *"G" (general
audiences)** content rating. This means:
- adverts are *never personalised*, and are not based on your interests or on what you do in other
  apps or websites;
- Google limits the information it collects and uses for these adverts, as its policies for
  child-directed requests require.

To show adverts, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process limited technical
information such as your device's IP address, device type and operating system, information about the
app, and the device's advertising identifier. Google does not use this information to build a profile of
you or to personalise adverts in a child-directed request. For more about how Google handles this
information, see:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can stop all full-screen adverts by buying *Remove Ads*.

## Purchases
The game offers optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads, and packs of extra lives). These are processed by
*Google Play*. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Google Play tells the game
only which product was bought, so it can be given to you. Purchases are subject to Google Play's terms
and to any parental controls set on the device or Google account. To manage these, see
https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/1075738

## Device permissions
The game asks for these permissions:
- *Internet and network access*: to load adverts and to make and restore purchases.
- *Vibration*: for small vibrations during play. You can turn these off in the game's settings.
- *Google Play Billing*: to make purchases.
- *Advertising ID*: declared by Google's advertising software. In this game, adverts are always
  requested as child-directed and are not personalised.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages, and we treat every player as a child. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game has no way for players to
share personal information with us or with other players. If you are a parent or guardian and have any
questions, please contact us using the details below.

## Your rights
Because we do not collect or hold any personal information about you, we have nothing to give you,
correct or delete. Information saved by the game stays on your device and you can remove it at any time
(see above). If you have questions about information processed by Google for adverts or purchases, you
can contact Google through the links above. If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to
complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office:
https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes to this policy
If we change how the game uses information, we will update this page and the effective date above.
