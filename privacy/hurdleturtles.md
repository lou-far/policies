# Privacy policy

*Hurdle Turtles* is a small single-player game. This page explains what it stores and what its
adverts and purchases involve.

## What the game stores on your device
Your best score, your settings, whether you have seen the how-to-play screen, any extra lives you have
bought, and whether you have removed adverts. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. We have no
accounts and no analytics.

## Adverts
The game can show adverts using **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID,
IP address and information about the adverts it shows, to serve and measure adverts (and personalised
ones, if you allow it). Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and elsewhere), you are shown Google's
consent form before any advert is loaded, and you can change your choice later. See
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads for how Google uses this data.

You can remove all full-screen adverts with the **Remove Ads** purchase.

## Purchases
Purchases are made through **Google Play** (and, in future, the **App Store**). We never see your
payment details; the store handles payment and tells the game only which product was bought.
